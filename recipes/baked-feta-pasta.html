Baked Feta Pasta

Fusilli in Feta with Tomatoes

This is one of my favorite dishes. It is ease to prepare and a culinary masterpiece that is ready in no time. Place the feta and tomatoes in a baking dish with plenty of olive oil and herbs, then bake in the oven. Meanwhile, cook the pasta; I recommend fusilli as the sauce clings to it better, but any pasta shape will work.

Mix both together and: Buon appetito!
